Agentic AI: A Glimpse into the Unknown:

Agentic AI refers to AI systems capable of making independent decisions. We're not talking about small, insignificant choices but substantial decisions that have the potential to influence outcomes in real-world scenarios. The concept of Agentic AI nudges us towards a future where artificial entities function with an autonomy comparable to human agents.

Is Agentic AI a reality?

The pathway towards completely autonomous AI is being paved, but we aren't quite there yet. Current AI systems relying on Machine Learning and Deep Learning techniques still require human involvement at various stages. Continuous supervision, data training, and tweaking of algorithms all call for a human hand. However, research is ongoing to build AI systems capable of self-learning and self-adapting, that's where Agentic AI enters the scene.

Future Possibilities:

Agentic AI could encompass numerous applications. Imagine self-driving cars that not only maneuver themselves on the road but also decide which passenger to pick up or choose the best route based on current traffic scenarios and energy utilization. Consider fully automated smart factories that make their own decisions about production schedules, handling machine maintenance, and resolving bottlenecks in processes.

Potential Pitfalls:

Like any technological advancement, Agentic AI comes with its own set of challenges. The most significant apprehension surrounding Agentic AI is about accountability. If an AI makes a decision leading to undesirable results, who is held accountable? This poses ethical and legal challenges that society must reconcile with. It’s not just about building the technology but also about devising a framework within which it operates safely and responsively.
